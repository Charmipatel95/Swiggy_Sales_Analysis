# 🍕 Swiggy Sales Analysis — Python Data Analysis Project

An end-to-end data analysis project built using Python to analyze Swiggy food delivery sales data. The project focuses on customer ordering behavior, revenue trends, restaurant performance, and regional demand patterns using data cleaning, KPI analysis, and visualization techniques.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Food delivery platforms generate thousands of orders daily, producing massive amounts of transactional data. The challenge is transforming this raw data into meaningful business insights that help understand:

- Which states and cities generate the highest revenue?
- Which restaurants and dishes perform best?
- How customer ordering behavior changes over time?
- What food categories contribute the most to revenue?
- How urban working-class populations influence food delivery demand?

This project analyzes Swiggy order data using Python to answer these business questions through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data visualization.

# 💡 Key Findings & Insights

## 1. Karnataka Generated the Highest Revenue
Karnataka recorded the highest revenue among all states, driven primarily by Bengaluru — one of India’s largest IT and working-professional hubs.

---

## 2. Bengaluru Emerged as the Top-Performing City
Bengaluru generated the highest sales among all cities. Metropolitan and working-class regions showed the strongest demand for online food delivery.

---

## 3. Fast-Food Chains Dominated Revenue
KFC, McDonald’s, Pizza Hut, Burger King, and Domino’s generated a major share of total platform revenue, reflecting strong urban demand for fast-food meals.

---

## 4. Veg Orders Dominated Volume, Non-Veg Generated Higher Value
Veg food contributed the majority of order volume, while Non-Veg customers showed higher spending per order on average.

---

## 5. Most Popular Dish Was Not the Most Profitable
Some dishes appeared frequently in order volume charts but contributed less revenue compared to premium dishes with higher pricing.

---

## 6. Revenue Trends Remained Stable
Monthly and weekly revenue trends remained consistent throughout the analysis period, indicating a strong repeat-order customer base.

---

## 7. Weekend Sales Were Slightly Higher
Saturday recorded the highest revenue while Tuesday recorded the lowest, but the variation remained relatively small.

---

## 8. Service Quality Stayed Consistent
Average ratings remained stable across quarters, indicating reliable customer satisfaction levels throughout the year.
